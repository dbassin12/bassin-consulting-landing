How I use AI

AI drafts. I decide.

AI makes the first version of the game, the video, or the worksheet. It is fast, and it is a starting point. A teacher makes the call.

On my school's AI Task Force, I coach teachers in group demos and one-on-one, and I helped write our AI guidelines.

Sample AI guidelinesA one-hour workshop plan

  • Check it like a grader

    I check facts against real sources, and I check every number and every picture. As a first reader on 87 essays, AI scored one a 79 that earned a 96.

  • Student privacy first

    Student names come out before anything goes to AI. My games have no student accounts, and scores stay on the device.

  • Built for a real school day

    38-minute periods, shared Chromebooks, a black-and-white printer, and students with IEPs. If a tool does not work there, it is not done.

  • Leave the thinking to people

    The tool does the busywork. Students still draw the graph and write the why. Teachers still make the call.
