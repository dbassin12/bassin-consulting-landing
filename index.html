A pergola construction company

Talk into the phone, and the job is in the system

After a call, the owner records a voice note. AI writes it down, pulls out the names, addresses, and job details, matches existing customers, and saves everything to the database. No paperwork at the end of the day.

  • Voice notes
  • Whisper
  • Gemini
  • A real database

A plumbing company

A phone call becomes a CRM deal in under a minute

When a call ends, AI writes down what was said and pulls out the job, the address, how urgent it is, the price quoted, and the next step. A new deal shows up in the CRM, built to work with the phone number and CRM a business already has.

  • Twilio
  • Whisper
  • An LLM
  • Pipedrive

A solo law practice

New case details, pulled in automatically

An AI workflow that pulls details from emails, phone notes, and intake forms into the case management system, so nobody spends half a day copying them by hand.

What the workflow does

  1. Reads a new email, phone note, or intake form
  2. Pulls out the details the case needs
  3. Puts them into the case management system
  4. The attorney's team checks it and moves on

A university financial aid office

FinAid Dashboard

Built with a financial aid counselor. The federal aid formula misses regional cost of living and K-12 tuition. This dashboard adjusts for both with real economic data and shows each step of the math, so the counselor can explain every number.

  • Next.js
  • TypeScript
  • Real economic data
